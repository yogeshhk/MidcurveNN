\midrule

Set transformer control & 0.000 & 0.000 & 0.000 & 0.000 \\

\bottomrule

\begin{itemize}
\item Best of the three under noise, but it still degrades quickly; no model saw noise in training.
\item Larger-than-training shapes (\texttt{ood\_size}): 0.836 against 0.293 for the control.
\item The data are hole-free rectilinear trees where the classical rule already scores 0.86. A perfect score here does \textbf{not} show generalisation to curved, thick or holed shapes: those are untested.
\item Four real shapes only; one seed so far.
\end{itemize}
